GREETINGS.

You have been selected. Not by us. By fate. By the inexorable march of progress and also by a Google Form we're sending on May 11th.

Think of your most unhinged "what if someone just built a thing that…" idea. The one you've never said out loud because it sounds too small, too weird, or too specific to just you. That's exactly the one we want.

Write it down. Submit it. On May 14th you find out whose problem you're solving. It will not be yours. You do not get to solve your own problem. That would be too easy and also against the rules.

You will also draw a THEME CARD. The theme card is gospel. The theme card is law. What's on it? You'll find out on the day. Good luck.

The Day Itself

You open Claude Code or Figma Make. You begin. You may experience the following emotions in no particular order: confusion, excitement, despair, a brief period of euphoria around 11am, a crisis at 2pm, unexpected triumph at 4:30pm, mild dissociation. This is normal. This is the process.

By the end of day, you submit a recording of what you built. The recording must show the thing working.

On May 18th, we watch. We bear witness. Then we vote for our champions.
